## GSE Health Board Requirements
REQ-01 — Protocol Translation
The GSE health monitoring board shall translate messages bidirectionally between CAN FD and Ethernet, preserving message content and timing integrity in both directions.

The GSE health monitoring board shall measure the ambient temperature within the electronic panel enclosure.
